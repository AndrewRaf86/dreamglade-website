Getting there and back

From Iquitos to the land — and home again.

You make your own way to Iquitos. From the moment you land, here is exactly what happens next — and how you get home again.

  1. 01 / Fly in

    Land in Iquitos

    You arrange your own flights. We recommend arriving 1–2 nights early to rest and avoid a delayed flight risking the transfer.

  2. 02 / Meeting point

    City meeting point, 1pm

    On your first retreat day, we meet you at a designated point in Iquitos — no public route runs to the centre.

  3. 03 / The drive

    ~1hr by 4x4

    Past Moralillo, toward Nauta. Included in your stay — arranged by us, not booked separately.

  4. 04 / Arrival

    Settle into your tambo

    Meet the team, see your tambo, find your bearings. The first ceremony is held that night.

  5. 05 / Departure

    Transfer home

    A final circle, then a group transfer to Iquitos. Airport drop-off is included for flights after 3pm.

Prototype behavior: the line's length is measured at runtime (path.getTotalLength()) and drawn in via stroke-dashoffset — a paint-only SVG property, not a layout property — triggered once by IntersectionObserver. Stage dots and the five text cards below fade/lift in afterward. This turns four paragraphs of transport logistics (airport pickup, cash-in-soles advice, 1pm cutoff, drop-off timing) into one glance-able sequence; the full prose detail still lives on the What to Expect and FAQ pages for anyone who wants it — this doesn't replace that text, it previews it.
