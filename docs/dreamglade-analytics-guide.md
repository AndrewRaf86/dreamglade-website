# Dreamglade Website Analytics — A Guide for Wade & Clarisa

This is a plain-English guide to the analytics now running on dreamglade.com. No technical background needed.

## 1. What Vercel Analytics shows

Vercel Analytics (built into our hosting) shows how many people visit the site, which pages they look at, where they come from (Google, Instagram, direct link, etc.), and what device/country they're using. This is all automatic — no setup needed to see it.

On top of that, we've added a small number of **custom events** — specific clicks we want to know about, like someone clicking "Apply" or checking an availability date.

## 2. What custom events were added

| Event name | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Apply Click** | Someone clicked a button/link to start an application (hero, nav menu, or pricing section) |
| **Availability Click** | Someone clicked "Verify with Paul" or "Ask About This Retreat" on a specific date range |
| **Email Click** | Someone actually reached the step of emailing booking@dreamglade.com |
| **Safety Click** | Someone clicked through to the full Safety & Preparation page |
| **FAQ Click** | Someone clicked through to the full FAQ page |
| **Experience Click** | Someone clicked through to the "What to Expect" page |
| **YouTube Click** | Someone clicked a YouTube link (channel or guest video) |
| **Instagram Click** | Someone clicked the Instagram link |
| **Google Reviews Click** | Someone clicked through to read Google reviews |

Each event also notes roughly *where* on the site it happened (e.g. "hero", "pricing", "availability") so you can tell which part of the page is driving action.

## 3. How to open the dashboard

1. Log in to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and open the **Dreamglade** project.
2. Click the **Analytics** tab near the top.
3. Scroll down to the **Events** panel — this is where the custom clicks (Apply Click, Email Click, etc.) show up.

## 4. What each event means, in plain terms

- **Apply Click** = interest. Someone is thinking about coming.
- **Email Click** = a real lead. Someone finished the steps and is about to email us.
- **Availability Click** = someone is checking a specific date range — useful for seeing which retreat windows get the most attention.
- **Safety / FAQ / Experience Click** = someone is doing research before deciding. More of these usually means people are seriously considering it, not just browsing.
- **YouTube / Instagram / Google Reviews Click** = someone wants social proof — reviews or real footage — before trusting us.

## 5. What to check weekly

A quick weekly glance is enough:

1. Total visitors this week vs. last week.
2. Number of **Apply Clicks** and **Email Clicks** — are people starting and finishing the inquiry step?
3. Which **Availability Click** date ranges are getting attention.
4. Any big spike or drop compared to a normal week.

## 6. How to interpret the numbers

- A rise in visitors after posting on Instagram/YouTube = that content is working.
- More Apply Clicks than Email Clicks is normal — not everyone who starts finishes right away.
- A specific date range getting a lot of Availability Clicks may mean it's popular, or it may mean people are confused/checking if it's open — worth a look either way.

## 7. What not to overreact to

- **Day-to-day swings.** Traffic naturally jumps around. Look at weekly trends, not single days.
- **A slow week.** One quiet week doesn't mean something is wrong — retreat inquiries are seasonal and often driven by word of mouth.
- **Low numbers on a brand-new page.** New content (like a new FAQ answer) takes time to get found and clicked.

## 8. Privacy note

No personal, medical, medication, or application details are ever tracked. The events only record that a click happened and roughly where on the site — never names, emails, phone numbers, message content, or health/safety answers.

## 9. Suggested weekly report format

A simple copy-paste template for a weekly check-in:

```
Week of [date]:
- Visitors: [number] (vs. [last week's number])
- Apply Clicks: [number]
- Email Clicks: [number]
- Most-clicked availability date: [date range]
- Notes: [anything unusual — a spike, a quiet stretch, a popular post]
```
